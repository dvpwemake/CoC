Who we are

Chronicle of Convergence (CoC) is a digital cultural magazine and social media channel exploring the venues where culture, society, and technology intersect in American life—specifically museums, cafés, and churches. We don’t just compile destinations; we inspire people to view these points of convergence (human vs. artificial intelligence, heritage vs. innovation, arts vs. technology) through a deeper lens. Our episodes are chronicle-style field guides, avoiding lazy listicles or “best of” rankings. Human voice only; the host is never on camera; the subject venue is the focus.

Why your church

  • A living congregation — not a backdrop-only landmark ranking
  • A room with a real practice: congregation, services, location
  • A story neighbors and visitors can recognize without hype

What we are not

  • Not a hard-sell ad or paid “#ad” placement by default
  • Not a review score, roast, or gotcha interview
  • Not a multi-crew disruption of worship or peak service hours

The ask — one appointment

We request a short, scheduled visit to obtain venue permission and, if you agree, a brief on-site or off-site interview with clergy, facilities, communications, or your designated spokesperson.

  • Venue: photograph and film exterior and agreed public areas only (and agreed details only) for editorial use in the CoC sacred-rooms series
  • Interview: 10–20 minutes — who the congregation is, when services open the doors, how location shapes the room, who the room is for
  • Outcome we want: a calm 15–30 minute window on the calendar, not a cold walk-in during service

How we work on site

Crew Solo host-producer (one person) · handheld · no flash · no tripod unless you approve
Timing Prefer weekday mid-morning or mid-afternoon outside service times, funerals, and major feast preparation. Weekend only if pre-booked by your office.
Duration Venue capture typically 20–40 minutes · Interview 10–20 minutes · Total often under one hour
People No worshipper faces without consent · staff/clergy only with permission · host never appears on camera
Credit On-screen and/or description credit for the church or parish by name (and website if you wish)

What you get

  • Respectful portrayal in a cultural series — not a meme edit
  • Named credit to an audience that values craft and place
  • A simple record of permission (email confirmation)

What we need from you

  • Yes / no on venue photography & ambient film
  • Yes / no on interview (who, when)
  • Any no-photo zones or pastoral rules
  • Preferred off-peak window this month
  • Your church's best story
Next step

A quick call or reply by email to set a date. If the fit is mutual, we send a one-line permission confirmation and arrive only at the agreed time.
