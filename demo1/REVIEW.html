01 · Why

  • Live site works, but still reads partly like an ops console rather than a cultural daily.
  • Editorial and Field signals fight for equal weight; cover story should lead.
  • Recent share/icons and image-dedup fixes are solid; hierarchy and rhythm need a system pass.
  • IT Design desk (2026-07-29) owns the bar; Web eng implements only after review.

02 · North star

Feel like a living American desk: warm paper, quiet gold, serious type — a modern cultural daily, not a SaaS dashboard. Keep scan speed; raise read dignity.

03 · What stays locked

Gold-first paper palette · serif editorial voice · Host never on camera · Insight grammar convergence of A and B · Path S free stack · parallel path only until principal cutover (no production overwrite).
