On Friday, September 11, 2026, we hold that park to one pressure: what still ages in the body when the room is a celebration, and when the industry asks if it is legal to slow down.

After an Anthropic researcher quit, warning the race is gambling with our lives. NASA is standing up a United States Space Academy, less than two weeks after the executive order. A pavilion in the park. A liver that looks older than the child. A slowdown that may not be allowed. Convergence is not the building that celebrates the painter. It is whether the body is still counted when the room is already named.
